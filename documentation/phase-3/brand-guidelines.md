## 1. Brand Positioning & Purpose

### Brand Positioning Statement
> **SAHAYAK** is India's sovereign, dignified, and intelligent digital welfare companion. It Bridges the gap between the Government of India's statutory social safety nets and 800+ million vernacular-first citizens through voice-driven clarity, zero bureaucratic jargon, and bulletproof document verification.

### Core Value Proposition
- **For Citizens**: Radical clarity on rightful government entitlements, verifiable eligibility in under 3 minutes in their mother tongue, and absolute protection against predatory middlemen (*dalals*).
- **For Families & Digital Proxies**: A unified, dependable cockpit to verify, apply for, and track social benefits for parents, siblings, and children.
- **For Grassroots Administrators (CSCs & Panchayats)**: Pre-verified citizen dossiers via tamper-proof QR tokens that eliminate data-entry errors and establish statutory fee compliance.

## 3. Logo & Visual Mark Direction

### The "Ashoka Chakra & Helping Hand" Fusion: The SAHAYAK Logomark
- **Conceptual Geometry**:
  - A stylized, geometric circular seal composed of two interlocking concentric paths:
    1. An outer 24-spoke stylized arc evoking the constitutional authority of the *Ashoka Chakra* and sovereign protection.
    2. An inner open upward palm motif forming the letter **'S'** that seamlessly transitions into a speech wave (representing Bhashini voice assistance).
- **Bilingual Typographic Lockup**:
  - Primary Logotype: **SAHAYAK** set in a modern, humanist sans-serif with geometric roundness (customized *Inter Display* with subtle ink traps).
  - Vernacular Counterpart: Sub-lockup rendering **"ಸಹಾಯಕ"** in Kannada (*Noto Sans Kannada Bold*) and **"सहायक"** in Devanagari (*Noto Sans Devanagari Bold*) with matched optical x-height.
- **Clearance & Minimum Sizing**:
  - Minimum clearance zone: Equal to the height of the letter 'S' (1x) on all four sides.
  - Minimum digital reproduction size: **28px height** for mobile app bars; **18px** for favicon with isolated central glyph.

### Iconographic Rules:
- **Grid Baseline**: 24 × 24 dp base grid with a 2px keyline stroke weight, 1.5px corner radiuses.
- **Always Accompanied by Text**: In citizen-facing flows, an icon is **never presented in isolation**; it always pairs with a bold vernacular label.
- **Optical Weight Matching**: Kannada and Devanagari characters carry more optical ink weight than Latin characters; iconography is drawn with slightly heavier optical center-of-mass (2px stroke) to harmonize with Indic headlines.

## 5. Visual Language & Design Principles

1. **Precision of Linear, Warmth of Bharat**:
   - Clean, razor-sharp 1px border dividers (`#E2E8F0`), micro-radius corners (8px–12px), and strict 4px/8px rhythm inspired by Linear and Stripe, combined with the warm, earthen tones of rural India.
2. **Zero "AI Slop" or Unnecessary Glassmorphism**:
   - Avoid blurry frosted glass overlays, illegible floating gradients, and neon shadows that wash out in harsh sunlight.
   - Use crisp, opaque surfaces with solid, high-contrast borders and purposeful micro-elevations.
3. **Statutory Fee Transparency Banners**:
   - Every financial entitlement and service is branded with an unmissable **Statutory Seal**: green for 100% Free Government Entitlement, amber for capped CSC charges (₹30.00).

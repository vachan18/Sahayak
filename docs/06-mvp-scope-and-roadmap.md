## 2. Benchmark Showcase Schemes (Strictly Verified, No Fabrication)

To ensure unassailable authenticity during evaluation, the prototype centers on **5 official, high-impact national schemes** across distinct life stages and demographic groups.

### Scheme 1: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (Government of India)
- **Benefit**: ₹6,000 per year transferred in 3 equal four-monthly installments of ₹2,000 directly into the bank accounts of landholding farmer families.
- **Eligibility Criteria**:
  - Small and marginal farmer families with cultivable landholding in state land records (*Bhulekh*).
  - Exclusions: Institutional landholders, families with serving/retired government employees or income-tax payers.
- **Mandatory Documents**:
  1. Aadhaar Card (mandatory eKYC).
  2. Land record proof (*Khatauni / Khasra* or state land title).
  3. Bank Passbook / Account details (NPCI Aadhaar-seeded).

### Scheme 2: Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)
- **Ministry**: National Health Authority (NHA), Ministry of Health & Family Welfare
- **Benefit**: Cashless health cover of up to **₹5,00,000 per family per year** for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across 28,000+ empaneled public and private hospitals.
- **Eligibility Criteria**:
  - Automatically covers bottom 40% vulnerable population identified via Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC 2011) rural deprivation criteria (D1 to D7) and urban occupational categories, or active NFSA ration card holders.
- **Mandatory Documents**:
  1. Aadhaar Card or Voter ID.
  2. Ration Card or PM-JAY Family Letter / SECC slip.

### Scheme 3: Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Women and Child Development
- **Benefit**: Direct Cash Benefit of **₹5,000** in two installments for the first living child of the family (and ₹6,000 for a second girl child) to compensate for wage loss during pregnancy and promote safe institutional delivery.
- **Eligibility Criteria**:
  - Pregnant Women and Lactating Mothers (PW&LM) who are not regular employees with Central/State Govt or PSUs.
- **Mandatory Documents**:
  1. Mother-Child Protection (MCP) Card registered at Anganwadi / Primary Health Centre.
  2. Aadhaar of the mother and husband.
  3. Joint or single bank account passbook.

### Scheme 4: PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA)
- **Benefit**: Collateral-free working capital micro-loan: 1st tranche up to ₹10,000 (repayable in 1 year), 2nd tranche ₹20,000, 3rd tranche ₹50,000 with 7% interest subsidy and cashback on digital transactions.
- **Eligibility Criteria**:
  - Urban street vendors, hawkers, thela-walas operating in urban/peri-urban areas prior to March 2020 (or identified in local municipal surveys).
- **Mandatory Documents**:
  1. Certificate of Vending (CoV) / Identity Card issued by Urban Local Body (ULB) OR Letter of Recommendation (LoR).
  2. Aadhaar Card & Bank Account.

### Scheme 5: Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Castes / OBC Students
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment / State Social Welfare Depts
- **Benefit**: 100% tuition fee reimbursement and monthly maintenance allowance for students pursuing higher education (class 11 to post-graduate degrees).
- **Eligibility Criteria**:
  - Enrolled in recognized college/university.
  - Annual family income below ₹2.5 Lakh per annum.
- **Mandatory Documents**:
  1. Caste Certificate issued by authorized revenue authority (SDM/Tehsildar).
  2. Income Certificate for the current financial year.
  3. Previous year marksheet & College Fee Receipt.
  4. Aadhaar Card linked to bank account.
